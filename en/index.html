My method

Four steps, none of them optional.

The same approach, whether the project is a CRM, an automation or an AI model.

Step 01

Frame

Understand the business, its pain points and its data. Quantify the value before writing a single line of code.

Example: two AI studies stopped at an early stage — estimated ROI too low.
Step 02

Prototype

A fast POC, on your real data, validated by the people who will use it every day.

Example: Official Journal POC (regex + AI), validated by the business.
Step 03

Industrialise

Security, sovereignty, IT integration, go-live. The POC becomes a tool.

Example: DICTANOT, from POC to commercial launch.
Step 04

Support

Training, change management, support. A tool only has value if people use it.

Example: group-wide ticketing adopted by 100+ agents in 9 months.

FAQ

Before we talk.

What are your rates?

It depends on the format: a fixed price for an assessment or a well-defined automation, a daily rate for an interim project manager or Product Owner engagement. I send you a costed proposal after a first 30-minute call.

Where do you work?

On site in the South of France (Aix-en-Provence, Avignon, Marseille and surroundings) and remotely everywhere else. Both combine very well.

Full-time or part-time?

Both are possible: a full-time engagement, or a few days a week to lead a project alongside your teams.

How do you protect our data?

Confidentiality is part of how I work: I have designed data and AI projects handled entirely in-house, with no exposure to third-party services. An NDA can be signed from the very first conversation.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. The AI & Data assessment and process automation are designed to move fast, with a scope that fits your size and budget.

Which industries have you worked in?

Legaltech and notaries, defence, telecoms, public transport and retail. The method — start from the business need, prove the value, industrialise — works in any industry.

How do we start?

The easiest way: book a 30-minute call. We review your needs and I suggest the best-suited format, with no commitment.
